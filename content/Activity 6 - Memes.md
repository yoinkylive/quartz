> [!NOTE] Rationale
> Through this piece, I wanted to explore the aforementioned ideas of calibrated amateurism and participatory culture in my works. Memes in particular draw from an amateur aesthetic, the simplistic unrealistic style appeals to a sense of absurdism with the *Snapchat*-esque text placed on top appealing to a sense of intimacy as if this was sent directly to the viewer. 
> 
> In this meme, *Tung Tung Tung Sahur* is the focal point, animated to knock the laptop down shut implying that the person behind the screen cannot do their work. The background links to participatory culture, as it comes from a trend where people would be unable to perform simple tasks due to a character interrupting them. 
> 
> The intention from this meme was to draw from the shared experience of procrastinating on an assignment and finding an excuse as to why you were late, an experience that many university students have shared.
