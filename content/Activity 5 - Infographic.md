> [!NOTE] Rationale
> This infographic explains the concept of participatory culture within digital communication, targeting everyday social media users. The intention behind the piece is to demonstrate how audiences have shifted from passive consumers to active participants who create and share content online. This draws on Hartley's understanding of the audience being active participants in the production and circulation of meaning in digital media. 
> 
> This also pulls from Laurie Ouellette and Jonathan Gray's Keywords for Media Studies, they explain that media systems are shaped by institutions and platforms, not just users and cultural participation happens within structured environments. 
> This infographic aims to underscore the fact that power still exists in participatory spaces. 
> The circular "wheel design" visually represents the on-going cycle of consumption, creation and growth, emphasizing the nature of participatory culture. Minimal text and iconography was used to ensure clarity. 
>
