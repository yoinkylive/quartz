### Executive Summary
Discord's stakeholder mapping for the platform highlights a diverse ecosystem with varying levels of power and interest following major recent data breaches. provoking heightened concerns around data privacy and security. High interest, high power stakeholders; notably *executives* (10,9) and *governments* (10,7) are critical to the success of the businesses initiatives. These groups possess significant decision-making authority and regulatory influence requiring close management and ongoing interaction. 

Lower-power stakeholders such as employees (6, 9) and media organisations (6,4) play an important supporting role. Employees demonstrate a high interest due to their direct operational involvement in implementing security measures for the platform whereas media organisations hold a moderate amount of power in shaping public view, despite lower direct interest in the platform. 

Moderators (3,8), users (2,7), community-owners (4,10) and privacy advocacy groups (2, 9) represent key groups whose trust is essential. While they don't have any formal authority, their collective influence can significantly impact platform reputation and decrease user retention, which makes transparent communication strategies vital  

The general public (2,2) occupies a low-power, low-interest position but should be monitored for shifts in sentiment.

Overall, the stakeholder landscape underscores the need to balance regulatory compliance, internal coordination and emphasize user trust whilst developing an effective response to privacy concerns.

> [!NOTE] Rational
> This stakeholder mapping was developed for the online communication platform Discord in response to recent data-breaches and growing global concerns regarding user-data safety and surveillance. The purpose of this analysis is to underscore the need for user trust, and transparency. 
> 
> The stakeholder matrix identifies and evaluates key groups based on their levels of power and interest in relations to the platforms response. In this case, the high power stakeholders such as governments are prioritized due to their direct influence over legislation and regulatory outcomes. High-interest groups such as community owners are also deemed critical as their trust to the platform directly affects Discord's reputation and long-term sustainability. 
> 
> Lower power stakeholders such as the general public are still considered due to their ability to shape public perception. Employees are positioned moderately high in both power and interest due to their ability to implement higher security internally.
