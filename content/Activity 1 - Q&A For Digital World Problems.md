When it comes to surfing the internet, security is something that people should always be conscious of. Phishing, theft and scams are incredibly common on the modern internet. To browse safely, there are a few key considerations to make before downloading, entering card information or generally engaging with a website. 

> [!warning] Always Back Up Your Files
>  Backing up your files to a cloud provider is the easiest way to prevent any real damage from being done whilst you are engaging with the internet. 
>  Services such as Google Drive, OneDrive and iCloud Drive provide complimentary storage for signing up. 
> 

1. **How do I know that people aren't snooping my internet traffic?** 
	Key considerations to take in whilst browsing the web securely are ensuring the website you are connecting to uses Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (*HTTPS*). This is immediately noticeable by paying attention to the 🔒 in your address bar. Sites with this symbol encrypt your traffic, meaning you can safely enter your information without the risk of people spying on your network traffic. 

2. **Confirming the URL of the website you are visiting.**
	Phishing is an increasingly common method of scams that is denoted by a few key characteristics. *Notably, the domain name is similar visually to a real websites domain, and it's website layout is practically indistinguishable to the naked eye.* 
	
	If you are uncertain about the legitimacy of a website, sites such as [Virus Total](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/) can scan the URL for you and confirm it without having to access it yourself. 
	
	*For emails:*
	- Check the sender’s email address carefully
	- Hover over links to preview where they lead
	- Look for signs of urgency or unusual requests
	
	Some email services also allow you to expand sender details for more information before trusting the message.

3. **Password Leaks and Security Breaches, how can I protect myself from them?**
Password leaks and data breaches are common online security risks. Using a password manager such as [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com) helps by generating strong, unique passwords for each account and alerts you if your details appear in a breach. 

> [!DANGER]If I feel that my computer has gotten infected, what are the immediate steps I need to take?
>  - Immediately cut internet access from the device.
>  - Back up any sensitive documents that you have remaining on the device using a USB flash-drive or SD card. 
>  - Factory reset your mobile device / computer
>  - When restored, scan the remaining sensitive documents on the device and re-instate them if they come clean.
