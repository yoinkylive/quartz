### Context and Audience: Scienceworks
- **The Vibe:**
	- The exhibition is placed in Spotswood Pumping Station, blending it's industrial heritage with futuristic innovation. 
- **The Audience:** 
	- Targeted at families and school-aged children alongside school groups. 
- **Design Language:** 
	- The exhibition will focus on soft, water-y blue tones with rounded edges emulating something akin to a Frutiger Aero aesthetic, reminiscent of the design language the software of the era the exhibition is meant to focus on, contrasted by tones of orange, brown and silver to represent the industrialism of the hardware that backed it.
### "A History of Communications"
To fit in with Scienceworks, the exhibition should expect to have intractable installations that allow people to understand through demonstration rather than articulation. 
#### Potential Content
  - Hardware that defined the era, computers, mobile devices and MP3 players from the early 2000s. Show how revolutionary the interoperability of devices in this era was and how reliant we were on them.
  - Explore the 'backend' of these devices, tearing apart cables and devices to reveal the silicon and emphasize what made them tick. 
  - Demonstrate the issues that were faced in the era that we might not encounter in this day-in-age.

> [!NOTE] Rational
> This moodboard explores the visual identity and atmosphere of an interactive exhibition titled "A history of Communication", designed for Scienceworks. The target audience includes families and school-aged children, the concept emphasises this by focusing on a hands-on and visually intuitive experience. 
> 
> The moodboard draws inspiration from early 2000s digital design, using the soft blue tones, gradients and rounded forms to evoke a nostalgic, "frutiger aero"-esque aesthetic.
> 
> These elements will be contrasted by metallic textures, industrial materials and settings to reflect the hpysical infrastructure behind communication technologies, responding to the industrial setting of *Spotswood Pumping Station*.
> 
> Content inspiration includes early computers, mobile phones, MP3 players and networking hardware alongside torn-apart compontents to reveal internal systems. The moodboard emphasises interactivity, showing how young children may play with the technologies through touch and experimentation. 
> 
> The concept aims to "show, not tell" by visually communicating the evolution of technologies in a way that immerses them, rather than directly "informing" them.
